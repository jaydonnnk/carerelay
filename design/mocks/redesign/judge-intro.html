What happens in the demo

Four steps, about three minutes. Colour shows who is acting at each step.

  1. Mei

    She reads her plan

    What to do, by when, and who acts. Large text, one action at a time.

  2. Tencent ADP

    She says it back

    In her own words. ADP copies out what she said about the action, the time and who acts.

  3. CareRelay code

    Code checks it

    Fixed rules compare her phrases with the plan. If she said 8pm, it asks about the time.

    The booking fails

Where Tencent ADP is used

ADP reads language. CareRelay's code makes every decision about the plan. That split is the safety design.

Tencent ADP does

  • Read the words Mei confirmed
  • Copy out her action, time and who acts
  • Flag doubt, “won't”, “or” and changed minds
  • Refuse medical questions and injected instructions

Tencent ADP never

  • Sets the plan, the deadline or how urgent it is
  • Grades whether Mei understood
  • Books, sends or closes anything
  • Gets a second try if its reply is wrong
