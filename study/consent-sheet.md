# Taking part in this study

Form `consent-v1`.

Please read this, or have it read to you, before anything else happens. You do
not have to take part.

---

## What this is

This is a short study about a tool that shows the status of a care booking. We
want to know whether people can keep track of a plan from it.

The scenario is made up. The clinic, the name and the booking are fictional.
Nothing in this study is medical care, and nothing in it is medical advice.

## What will happen

- You will be shown a plan, either on a screen or on a printed card.
- You will be asked a few questions about it.
- It takes about ten minutes.
- Nothing is recorded as audio or video.

## What we write down

- Your answers to the questions, word for word.
- How long the reading took.
- How hard it was to keep track of the plan, which you rate from 1 to 5.
- Anything you tell us about how the session felt.

## What we do not write down

- No name, no address, no phone number, no email.
- No health information.
- No audio and no video.

The record is kept under a number, such as `d1`, not under your name.

## Stopping

You can stop at any point, for any reason, and you do not have to say why.
Anything already written down is marked as withdrawn and is not used.

## Keeping and destroying the record

The records are destroyed 30 days after the study is submitted. This paper form
stays with the facilitator until then and is destroyed with the rest.
