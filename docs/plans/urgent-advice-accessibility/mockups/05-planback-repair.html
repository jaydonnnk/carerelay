Two rounds is the limit. A third attempt turns a comprehension check into an interrogation, and a patient who cannot restate the plan needs a person, not another prompt.

Recorded outcome: not_recalled — then route to the approved human or emergency path. This is an honest result, reported as such, never dressed up as understanding.

Rules this screen enforces
  • The model interprets; code decides. The LLM extracts fields. equivalent() is a boolean over a time bucket and a closed action vocabulary.
  • Confirmed before compared. A transcript is never evaluated before the patient confirms or corrects it.
  • Field-scoped repair. Correct fields are left alone.
  • Bounded. Two rounds, then a human path. Never an infinite loop.
  • Hint level recorded. A restatement achieved at H2 or H3 is not evidence of recall.
  • Urgent guidance is never delayed by this screen. A 995-level recommendation appears immediately and does not wait for read-back.
