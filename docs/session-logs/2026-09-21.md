## Follow-up — applied to PLAN.md, plus the hint ladder

Jaydon asked for an obvious hint so an elderly user is prompted to restate the plan, and said to add both mechanisms to `PLAN.md`.

**Design tension resolved.** His example hint ("Remember! you have an appointment at 6pm today, at [place]") would have leaked the deadline and location — two of the three critical fields. A hint containing the answer turns PlanBack from a comprehension check into a reading test and invalidates the measurement. Resolution: a **four-level escalating hint ladder** where the *level used is always recorded*.

| Level | Patient sees | Recorded as |
|---|---|---|
| H0 | Question alone | `recall_unaided` |
| H1 | Three labelled slots: What · When · Who helps | `recall_scaffolded` |
| H2 | Plan card shown 5 s, then hidden | `recall_cued` |
| H3 | Plan shown, confirmed by choosing | `not_recalled` |

H0–H2 never reveal the critical fields. H3 is an honest outcome, not a failure. A persistent "show my plan" control means the patient is never trapped.

**Fixture rule added:** no asset may name a real healthcare facility. His example used a real hospital's psychiatric ward — excluded for privacy, reputational and simulation-honesty reasons. The fixture provider is now the fictional "Riverwood Community Clinic", labelled simulated everywhere.

## Follow-up 2 — the hint ladder as cognitive scaffolding

Jaydon asked to reframe the hinting feature as a way to help elderly users "keep their minds sharp" / tackle cognitive decline.

**Researched before writing, and the evidence blocked the direct version:**

| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| *Perspectives on Psychological Science*, 2022 | Meta-analyses show cognitive training produces **no far transfer** — gains stay on the trained task. |
| FTC, January 2016 | Lumosity/Lumos Labs paid **$2M** settling deceptive-advertising charges for implying its games "stave off" age-related cognitive decline. |
| *International Psychogeriatrics*, 2013 literature review | Spaced retrieval, errorless learning and vanishing cues carry Class II–III evidence for teaching **specific information** to people with memory impairment. |

**The reframe that works: adaptive scaffolding with fading.** The ladder is bidirectional — it escalates when the patient needs help and **fades when competence is demonstrated**. New plan starts at H1; unaided success lowers the start level next time; failure restores it; changes follow trends not single rounds; fading is invisible (no score, no streak, no progress bar). This is a real, citable cognitive-rehabilitation mechanism, so the ambition is preserved without a claim the evidence cannot support.

**Hard prohibitions added** to `PLAN.md` §2, §5.3, `01-product.md` and `00-status.md`: no cognitive improvement / "keep your mind sharp" / dementia prevention / screening / risk score / diagnostic output, anywhere in the product, submission, video or pitch. Also a language rule: never "cognitive rot," "brain training" or "use it or lose it" in anything user-facing.

**Why this mattered for scoring:** Responsible AI & Ethics is the project's highest dimension (6/10) and Impact is 5/10. An unsupported decline-prevention claim would not add points — it would undermine the one dimension the project is strongest on, and hand judges the most quotable attack on the submission.

**Longitudinal cognitive signal** recorded in `PLAN.md` §5.3 as an explicitly out-of-scope research hypothesis, so the ambition is not lost but is not presented in the submission. Population note added: pursuing cognitive monitoring would narrow the population to MCI / subjective cognitive decline and raise regulatory stakes materially.

## Four design principles + load-bearing audit

Jaydon set four principles for the idea: (P1) if it could be built without organiser tech, it's the wrong thing; (P2) be early — last month's limitation is this month's mainstream; (P3) align with the track theme; (P4) don't be afraid to be ambitious.

Wrote `docs/DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md` and linked it from `PLAN.md` §2 and `00-status.md`.

**Headline finding: by P1's own rule, the current scope is the wrong project — and `PLAN.md` already concedes it.** §2 says PlanBack and the Closure Contract "do not require WorkBuddy"; §3 says "another stack could reproduce the product."

**Audit result: 2 of 9 organiser surfaces carry real weight** (the WorkBuddy coordinator; MCP tool calls + their failure events). CodeBuddy is build-only. Six are unused: managed scheduler, Agent Runtime concurrency, durable cross-day sessions, TRTC voice, ADP guardrails/HITL, Miora.

**Root cause identified:** the §7 scope cuts removed *every longitudinal surface*. The plan kept the one-shot conversation and dropped everything after it — which is exactly where the platform becomes irreplaceable.

**The killer argument (organiser's own text):** Scenario 1 says "re-checks every 8 hours"; Scenario 2 says "daily logs for 10 days… compared to baseline"; the Challenge paragraph says "monitor changes that warrant escalation"; the population is older adults + caregivers + vulnerable groups. Comparing to a baseline **requires memory across days** — a conversation cannot do it. The organiser asked for the thing the platform uniquely supplies, and the plan cut it.

**P3 finding:** two of Challenge 1's seven behaviours are both weakly covered *and* exactly where organiser tech is load-bearing — **calibrated abstention** (needs ADP guardrails/HITL) and **dynamic reassessment** (needs scheduler + durable sessions). Same gap, two directions.

**P2 finding:** the *recovery* axis CareRelay sits on was taken by Luma (15 Sep 2026) and Hippocratic AI (13 Aug 2026). The open frontier is **abstention** — MedQAbstain (ACL 2026, July) showed LLMs systematically overcommit. Everyone races to automate more; the early move is to build the refusal and measure it.

**Important pushback recorded:** P1 taken literally is unsafe. The handbook requires "built on," not exclusivity [verified — `CHALLENGE_REQUIREMENTS_JUDGING.md` §8]. No rubric dimension rewards exclusivity; *AI Interaction* asks for depth, not impossibility. And account access is [unknown] with Gate A unrun — an exclusivity-dependent design may be undemonstrable. Recommended form: **at least one load-bearing axis, depth everywhere else.**

**Proposed reframe (not approved):** from "advice that closes" to **"a monitored episode that cannot lie."** Platform owns continuity and scale; we own clinical governance and truthfulness. PlanBack and the Closure Contract stay portable *on purpose*. New Gate C answer: "Remove WorkBuddy and the episode stops being reassessed and the cohort stops being monitored — you're left with a form."

**Noted tension:** P4 (ambition) collides head-on with the 16 Oct deadline (`PLAN.md` §7: 180–270 h total, 76–124 h for the new mechanisms alone). Net effort of the proposed changes is roughly neutral because restoring items 1–3 is offset by cutting PlanBack's baseline kill test.
